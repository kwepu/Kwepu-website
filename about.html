About KWEPU

Community-led environmental conservation, restoration and sustainable livelihoods in Rusinga, Homa Bay County, Kenya.

Who we are

Kamayoge Women Environmental Protection Unit (KWEPU) is a community environmental organization established in 2019 in Rusinga, Homa Bay County, Kenya. We work with communities and schools to protect the environment, restore landscapes and promote sustainable livelihoods.

Our vision

We envision greener, resilient and sustainable communities where people—especially women and young people—actively protect the environment, strengthen sustainable livelihoods and create lasting social and environmental impact.

Community-led conservation

We connect environmental restoration with practical local participation and stewardship.

Women & youth

We engage women and young people in environmental leadership, skills development and sustainable livelihoods.

Environmental education

We support practical environmental and climate learning in schools and communities.

Partnerships

We welcome local and international collaboration, knowledge exchange and volunteer service.
