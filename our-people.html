Volunteer · Mason

Philip Otieno

Philip’s story reflects the power of opportunity and determination. With KWEPU’s sponsorship support, he pursued masonry training and is now a mason. He continues to volunteer his services, giving back to the community that helped him move forward.

His journey is a reminder: empowerment can help someone build a livelihood—and become part of the change for others.
