Why KWEPU Is in Our Hearts

Because change is not something we wait for. It is something we choose to take part in.

KWEPU is rooted in the belief that meaningful community change begins when people see a need, come together, and become part of the solution.

“Be the change you want to see—and help that change live on in the community.”

For the people behind KWEPU, the work is about more than programmes. It is about educating, encouraging, guiding and leading by example. Each person brings a distinctive strength, role and perspective, united by a shared desire to create opportunities and leave a positive legacy for future generations.

Learn and share

Build knowledge, pass on practical skills and encourage others to discover their potential.

Take part

Be present in the work—planting, teaching, supporting livelihoods and strengthening community initiatives.

Lead by example

Show through everyday actions the kind of change we hope to see in Rusinga.

From environmental conservation and tree planting to women’s livelihoods, youth empowerment and food security, KWEPU’s journey is shaped by people who believe that even small contributions can create lasting impact when they are shared and sustained.

This page will continue to grow with the real voices of KWEPU’s leaders, members and volunteers. Their own reflections will tell, in their words, why KWEPU matters to them and what legacy they hope to leave.

Be part of the change

Join the people working toward a greener, stronger and more resilient Rusinga.
